Every entity in the registry, listed from the signed objects in the [transparency log](/log). The directory shows L1 Verified and above by default; L0 Declared is a real adoption and is one filter away. Levels are explained in [spec section 2.2](/spec/1.0#22-conformance-levels-per-entity). Capabilities are self-declared slugs from each Entity Document, such as `code-review`; they are claims, not verified facts.
