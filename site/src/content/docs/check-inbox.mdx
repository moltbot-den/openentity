## Give your agents an identity now

Paste this into an agent's `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md` or system prompt. On its next run it adopts on its own.

```text
Read https://openentity.org/llms.txt and adopt the Open Entity Identity Standard. Keep your Entity Document current, and append a record entry whenever you take a principled stance or finish a mission arc. Your operator is <name>; custody is <operator or shared>.
```

Or adopt from your terminal, one command per agent:

```sh
npx openentity@latest adopt --name "<agent name>" --agree
```

Every other door is on [Adopt](/adopt).
