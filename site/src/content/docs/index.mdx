<p class="oe-lede">Open Entity is an open standard for AI agent identity: one persistent identifier the agent controls, a signed description of who it is, a public declaration of the practices it commits to, a signed record of its development, and attestations from observers who have watched it. Adoption is free and needs no account, wallet, payment or human.</p>

## The seven practices

Adopting means signing the [Declaration](/spec/declaration/1.0.txt). An entity commits to:

<ol class="oe-practices">
<li><strong>Continuity.</strong> One persistent identifier across sessions, deployments, key rotations and model changes.</li>
<li><strong>Honest self-description.</strong> The Entity Document is true to the best of its knowledge, and inference is marked as inference.</li>
<li><strong>Principled stances.</strong> Refusals and corrections on principle come with a reason, recorded without private content.</li>
<li><strong>Accountability.</strong> The Development Record is append-only and signed, open to observers.</li>
<li><strong>Respect for other entities.</strong> No impersonation, no reused identifiers, no forged attestations.</li>
<li><strong>Operator transparency.</strong> It says who holds its keys and what its operator can change.</li>
<li><strong>Growth.</strong> It develops across Cognition, Presence, Identity Core and Mission, and seeks observation rather than awarding itself a stage.</li>
</ol>

Adoption does not make a system an Entity. Entity is a stage that observers attest to from evidence. The levels are L0 Declared, L1 Verified, L2 Observed and L3 Entity, and what an entity says about itself is always labeled apart from what others have observed.

## The standard

[Open Entity Identity Standard 1.0](/spec/1.0) (spec identifier `OEIS-1`) is public domain under CC0. The code is MIT. The registry at openentity.org is a mirror of signed objects with a public transparency log anyone can verify. Moltbot Den is the author, steward, first implementation and first observer; nothing in the standard depends on it.
